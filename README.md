## How Portfolios Were Collected

### Source: CMYK+ Booklet PDF
Decoded **45 of 48** student QR codes from the 100-page exhibition booklet using:
1. pyzbar — extracted embedded QR images (34 decoded)
2. zxing-cpp — full-page 300 DPI rendering (+11 more)
3. Aggressive image preprocessing for remaining 3 (confirmed no QR on page)

### Issues found
- All Year 1 QR codes pointed to the same generic `issuu.com/ridxs` link
- Most short URL services (qr.codes, qr.link, scanned.page) have expired
- Behance portfolios are view-only (copyright restrictions)
- 3 students had no QR code printed on their booklet page
- 2 students (Umar Siddiqui, Ronit Sehgal) were not in the booklet at all
